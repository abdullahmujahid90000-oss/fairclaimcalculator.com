The 17c Baseline Is a Comparison Tool, Not "True" Diminished Value

Where this site shows a "17c insurer-style baseline," that figure comes from a formula historically associated with a 2002 Muscogee County, Georgia class-action settlement mechanism — not a formula created by the Georgia Supreme Court, and not one approved or endorsed by any state insurance regulator as the definitive way to calculate diminished value. We show it, with its full arithmetic, purely as one transparent comparison point alongside a separate market-evidence worksheet. It is not a professional appraisal, and it is not evidence that a specific dollar figure is owed.
