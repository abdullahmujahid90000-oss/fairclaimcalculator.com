About FairClaimCalculator.com

How This Site Started

A few years ago, someone close to me was in a minor car accident — nobody seriously hurt, but enough medical bills and missed work to actually matter. A few weeks later, an insurance adjuster sent over a settlement offer: just a number and a deadline to accept it, with no real explanation of how they'd arrived at it. When I went looking for something that could explain the math behind an offer like that, I kept running into the same two options: dense legal-aid documents written for attorneys, or law firm blog posts clearly designed to get you to call and sign a retainer, with no real numbers shown anywhere in between.

That gap is what FairClaimCalculator.com exists to fill. The "multiplier method" used in the calculator here isn't some secret formula — it's a documented approach that insurance adjusters and personal injury attorneys have referenced for decades to arrive at a starting estimate. What was missing was a version of it anyone could actually use themselves: the reasoning shown step by step, the caveats made explicit, and no email address or account required just to see a result.

I built this site as a researcher and independent publisher, not as a law firm or an insurance company. Every article and calculator here is put together from publicly available consumer legal-education material — state bar association guides, insurance industry claims-handling literature, and general legal reference publications — and reviewed periodically for accuracy. Think of it as a well-organized starting point for understanding where you stand, not a replacement for a conversation with a licensed attorney in your state. Your state's specific negligence rules, the policy limits involved, and the details unique to your case can move a real number substantially — and that's exactly the kind of thing worth getting a professional's eyes on before you settle anything.

How We Research Our Content
