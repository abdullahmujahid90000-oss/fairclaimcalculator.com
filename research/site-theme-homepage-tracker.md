# Research: Site theme, homepage and My Claim tracker (topic 3, 2026-11-16)

## Search phrases (estimates; no keyword tool connected)
- Homepage: "car insurance claim calculator", "free car insurance claim calculators",
  "total loss and diminished value calculator".
- Tracker: "car insurance claim tracker", "how to track a car insurance claim",
  "how long does an insurance company have to respond to a claim".

## Competitors / references
- Claim trackers today are insurer apps (only for that insurer's claim) or paid spreadsheet
  templates (Gumroad) and form builders (MangoApps, Typeform). None is free, insurer-neutral and
  private with follow-up prompts.
- Claim log fields recommended across sources: claim number, date of loss, date filed, adjuster
  name and contact, policy number, deductible, estimate/supplements, offer, settlement, dated notes.
- Competitor homepages (The Zebra, Insurify) lead with quotes; few lead with the claim situation.

## Facts used
- NAIC Unfair Claims Settlement Practices model: acknowledge notice of a claim within 15 days;
  states vary (NAIC compiled chart lists 10 working days in some states, 15 in others, and separate
  accept/deny clocks). The tracker uses 15 days only as a "time to follow up" prompt and says
  "many states", never a specific state's deadline.

## Found and fixed
- Desktop navigation was invisible (details element closed with its toggle hidden).

## What we built that competitors don't have
- Free, private, insurer-neutral claim tracker with progress, next step, follow-up prompts,
  contact log, calendar (.ics) reminders, backup/restore, print.
