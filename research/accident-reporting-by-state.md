# Research: Accident reporting by state (topic 5, 2026-11-20)

## Search phrases (estimates; no keyword tool connected)
- "do I have to report a car accident in [state]", "[state] accident report form", "SR-1 California",
  "how to get a copy of a police report [state]", "how long do I have to report a car accident".

## Competitors
- State DMV / police pages rank first for each state but cover only their own agency.
- Law-firm pages list fees that are often outdated (Michigan $10 vs $15; Florida old fees; Texas mail requests).
- Third-party "report finder" sites charge far more than the official price.

## Facts confirmed this run (official sources, see crash-report-copies.ts)
- CA: CHP 190 to the CHP Area office; proper party of interest; ID or notarized; $10 for 1–25 pages.
- TX: TxDOT Crash Report Online Purchase System, CR-3; $6 regular / $8 certified.
- FL: Florida Crash Portal; $10 + $2 convenience fee (s. 321.23); 60-day confidentiality (s. 316.066),
  sworn statement HSMV 94010; up to ~10 days to appear.
- NY: DMV online $7 search + $15 per report; MV-198C by mail; NYPD for NYC crashes under 30 days.
- PA: PSP online or SP 7-0015; $22; available 15 days after the crash.
- IL: ISP E-Pay; $5 statutory + 2.35% card fee ($1 min); reconstruction reports $20 by mail.
- OH: ODPS crash retrieval (via City of Columbus guidance); often free; fee not confirmed officially.
- GA: GDOT repository; BuyCrash or GDOT mail form; GSP via DPS; fee not confirmed.
- NC: individuals by mail/in person with TR-67A (report DMV-349); online only for businesses; fee on form.
- MI: MSP Traffic Crash Purchasing System via MiLogin since 2026-04-14; $15 (House Fiscal Agency, Mar 2026).
