# Research: What to do after a car accident (topic 4, 2026-11-18)

## Search phrases and questions (estimates; no keyword tool connected)
- "what to do after a car accident", "what to do after a car accident not your fault",
  "car accident checklist", "car accident information sheet pdf", "documents needed for car insurance claim",
  "do I need to call the police after a minor accident", "should I move my car after an accident",
  "other driver has no insurance / drove away".

## Competitors read
- Insurers and comparison sites (Farmers, The Hartford, Progressive, Compare.com, The Zebra, Nolo, AAA):
  generic step lists; none interactive.
- Law-firm pages (most "not your fault" results): steer toward injury lawsuits.
- Gaps: almost no one explains state quick-clearance ("Steer It, Clear It") laws; no checklist you can
  tick off on a phone and carry into a claim record.

## Facts and sources used
- About half of states require drivers in minor, no-injury crashes to move drivable vehicles out of travel
  lanes: FHWA Traffic Incident Management Quick Clearance Laws; MoDOT "Steer It, Clear It"; ADOT "Minor
  crash? Move your car out of travel lanes".
- Replace a child car seat after a moderate or severe crash: NHTSA Car Seats and Booster Seats.
- State reporting rules: this site's 50-state accident-reporting directory (each entry sourced).
