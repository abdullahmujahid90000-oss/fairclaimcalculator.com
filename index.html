Check the Facts and Math Behind Your Auto Claim Offer

FairClaimCalculator is being rebuilt around a single job: helping U.S. vehicle owners audit a diminished-value or total-loss insurance offer, line by line, before deciding what to do next.

🔒 Free — no email or account required 🚗 Repaired-vehicle diminished value & total-loss offers 🛠️ Tools launching over the coming weeks

My Car Was Repaired

A vehicle can carry a documented accident history even after a perfect repair, and that history can lower resale value. Try the Diminished Value Calculator to see an insurer-style "17c" baseline next to real market evidence, side by side — never blended into one final answer.

My Car Was Declared a Total Loss

An actual-cash-value offer on a totaled vehicle is often off in a specific, checkable way: the wrong trim, the wrong mileage, thin or mismatched comparables. Try the Total-Loss Offer Audit to check the report facts and compare comps, or the Settlement Check Breakdown to see how the offer turns into a check.

Educational tool, not legal advice. FairClaimCalculator helps you organize facts, evidence, and arithmetic. It does not tell you what you are legally owed, determine fault, or replace a licensed attorney, independent appraiser, or your insurer's own review process. See our full disclaimer.

What's Coming Next

This site previously hosted a set of general personal-injury settlement calculators. We're rebuilding it as a focused resource for one specific problem: checking the facts and arithmetic behind a vehicle-value insurance offer. Older pages are being retired as the new tools go live — see the About page for the full explanation of why.

Tools are being built and published in this order:

  • Settlement Check Breakdown — live now. See exactly how an insurer got from a vehicle's value to the number on your check.
  • Total-Loss / ACV Offer Audit — live now. Compare the insurer's comparables and adjustments against your own evidence.
  • Evidence Packet & Claim Letter Builder — live now. Turn your audit into an editable, factual letter, plus an evidence checklist and local print/PDF export.
  • Diminished Value Calculator — live now. A transparent 17c-style baseline shown next to a separate market-evidence worksheet — never averaged into one number.

Every tool will run in your browser, keep your entries on your device by default, and show its full arithmetic — no hidden weights, no email gate, no guessed numbers presented as facts.
